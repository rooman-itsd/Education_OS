1. What is Education OS & What Are We Building Here?

The Core Pitch for Your Demo:

"Education OS is a modern, unified higher-education operating system designed to replace the dozens of disconnected legacy ERP tools that colleges and academies struggle with today. Instead of separate software for admissions, fees, hostel, attendance, exams, and sports, Education OS provides one single, multi-tenant cloud platform that adapts to any institution type through configurable profiles."

Architecture in Simple Terms

  1. Multi-Tenant by Design: Every database table enforces PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS) scoped strictly by organisation_id. A Sports College never sees a Medical College's records.
  2. Common Platform Foundations: Identity, Authentication, Tenancy, Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), Outbox Event Bus, Audit Ledger, and Central Notifications are shared across the whole system.
  3. Standard Suites (Common to all colleges): Academics, Examinations, Student Lifecycle, Finance & Operations, Campus Life, Facilities, and Governance.
  4. Specialized Suites (The Real Differentiator): Higher education isn't one-size-fits-all. A Sports College has completely different daily operational needs than an Engineering College. My domain is the Sports Suite: giving athletes, coaches, medical staff, and nutritionists professional-grade tools for performance, video analysis, fitness clearances, tournament travel, and skill rubrics.
  5. Security Pipeline: Every button and widget resolves in 4 clear steps: User → Role → Permission Keys → Organisation Plan Entitlement. Modules never check raw role names—they enforce fine-grained permissions.
