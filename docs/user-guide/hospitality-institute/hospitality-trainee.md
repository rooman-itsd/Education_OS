# Hospitality Trainee dashboard

You are a hospitality trainee. Your dashboard shows your classes and fees, your industry internship, practical
assessments, kitchen and lab bookings, events and job openings.

## What you see

| Panel | What it shows | When it has nothing |
|---|---|---|
| Today | Your classes today | Not built yet |
| Attendance summary | How much of your attendance is recorded | Not built yet |
| Upcoming assessments | Your next assessments and exams | Not built yet |
| Course materials | Notes, links and documents your teachers recently published in your courses | "No new course materials" |
| Fee dues | Fees you still owe | "No fees due" |
| Hostel notices | Notices for your hostel and for all hostels | "No hostel notices right now" |
| Transport notices | Delays, cancellations and route changes for your bus and all routes | "No transport notices right now" |
| Library loans | Books you have borrowed, with due dates and overdue ones marked | "No books on loan" |
| My industry internship | Your industry internship | Not built yet |
| My progress | Your latest level in each skill | "No assessments yet" |
| My bookings | The training kitchens and labs you have booked, coming up, with day, time and status | "No upcoming bookings" |
| Upcoming productions | The next banquets and events | "Nothing coming up" |
| My loans | Items you have borrowed, from awaiting approval to overdue | "Nothing on loan" |
| Job openings | Job openings you can apply to, those closing soonest first | "No openings right now" |

## If a panel says its data is not available

Some panels say that their data is not available yet, or that you do not have access. Usually it means that part of
Education OS has not been switched on for your organisation yet. Tell your organisation admin. A panel for a part your
organisation has not switched on may also be missing altogether. Panels that say "Not built yet" are not finished by
the Education OS team, and nothing is wrong with your account.

## What you can do

### Follow your industry internship

My industry internship shows where your industry internship stands. Log your hours there as you go.

### See your progress

My progress shows your latest level in each skill. Levels appear once an instructor has assessed you.

### Book a training kitchen or lab

A training kitchen or lab you book shows in My bookings with its day, time and status in words. Only training kitchens
and labs that your facility staff have set up can be booked. If the one you need is missing, ask the facility staff to
add it.

### Know what is coming

Upcoming productions lists the next ones with their dates.

### Track what you have borrowed

My loans shows each loan from awaiting approval to ready to collect, on loan, part returned or overdue. Return items
before the due date.

## Not sure?

Ask your teacher or supervisor about your work, the facility staff about bookings and loans, the accounts office about
fees, and your organisation admin about anything on your dashboard that looks wrong or missing.
