# Production Manager dashboard

You manage performances and their needs. Your dashboard shows the performance calendar, the next performances, low
stock of costumes and props, and today's bookings.

## What you see

| Panel | What it shows | When it has nothing |
|---|---|---|
| Production calendar | Performances scheduled over the coming weeks | "Nothing scheduled" |
| Upcoming productions | The next performances | "Nothing coming up" |
| Low stock | Costumes and props that have fallen to their reorder level or run out | "Stock is healthy" |
| Bookings today | Today's bookings of the practice studios you look after | "No bookings today" |

## If a panel says its data is not available

Some panels say that their data is not available yet, or that you do not have access. Usually it means that part of
Education OS has not been switched on for your organisation yet. Tell your organisation admin. A panel for a part your
organisation has not switched on may also be missing altogether. Panels that say "Not built yet" are not finished by
the Education OS team, and nothing is wrong with your account.

## What you can do

### Plan around the calendar

Production calendar lists performances with their dates over the coming weeks.

### Know what is coming

Upcoming productions lists the next ones with their dates.

### Reorder in time

Low stock lists costumes and props at or below their reorder level. Tell the person who raises purchase orders, or
raise one yourself if you can.

### See today's bookings

Bookings today lists the practice studios booked today, with counts, so you can have them ready.

## Not sure?

Ask your head or manager about the work itself, and your organisation admin if a panel is missing: your role decides
which panels you get.
