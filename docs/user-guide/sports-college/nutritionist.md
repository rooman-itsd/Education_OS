# Nutritionist dashboard

You plan nutrition for athletes. Your dashboard shows the athletes on plans, plan reviews that are due and body
composition results.

## What you see

| Panel | What it shows | When it has nothing |
|---|---|---|
| Athletes on plans | Athletes who have an active nutrition plan | "No athletes on a plan" |
| Plan reviews due | Nutrition plan reviews that are overdue or due this week | "No reviews due" |
| Body composition trend | Body fat, weight and other body measurements over time | "No body composition results yet" |

## If a panel says its data is not available

Some panels say that their data is not available yet, or that you do not have access. Usually it means that part of
Education OS has not been switched on for your organisation yet. Tell your organisation admin. A panel for a part your
organisation has not switched on may also be missing altogether. Panels that say "Not built yet" are not finished by
the Education OS team, and nothing is wrong with your account.

## What you can do

### See who is on a plan

Athletes on plans lists athletes whose nutrition plan is active.

### Keep reviews on time

Plan reviews due lists reviews that are overdue or due this week.

### Follow body composition

Body composition trend shows each measurement over time.

## Not sure?

Ask your head or manager about the work itself, and your organisation admin if a panel is missing: your role decides
which panels you get.
