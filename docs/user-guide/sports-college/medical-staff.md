# Medical Staff (Doctor / Physio) dashboard

You are a doctor or physiotherapist for the college's athletes. Your dashboard shows injured athletes, today's
appointments, fitness clearances and bookings.

## What you see

| Panel | What it shows | When it has nothing |
|---|---|---|
| Injured athletes | Athletes with an active or recovering injury, with expected return | "No injured athletes" |
| Appointments today | Physiotherapy, medical and nutrition sessions scheduled for today | "No appointments today" |
| Fitness clearances | Which athletes are cleared to train, with those not cleared or lapsing first | "No athletes yet" |
| Upcoming bookings | Coming bookings of the facilities you approve | "No upcoming bookings" |

## If a panel says its data is not available

Some panels say that their data is not available yet, or that you do not have access. Usually it means that part of
Education OS has not been switched on for your organisation yet. Tell your organisation admin. A panel for a part your
organisation has not switched on may also be missing altogether. Panels that say "Not built yet" are not finished by
the Education OS team, and nothing is wrong with your account.

## What you can do

### Track injuries

Injured athletes lists athletes with an active or recovering injury and their expected return date.

### Run today's appointments

Appointments today lists physiotherapy, medical and nutrition sessions with how many are scheduled and completed.

### Check clearances

Fitness clearances lists who is cleared to train, putting those not cleared or lapsing first.

### Approve bookings

Upcoming bookings lists coming bookings of the facilities you approve.

## Not sure?

Ask your head or manager about the work itself, and your organisation admin if a panel is missing: your role decides
which panels you get.
