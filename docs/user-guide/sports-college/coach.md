# Coach dashboard

You coach athletes and teach. Your dashboard shows your classes, your squad, training videos to review, facility
bookings and the tournament calendar.

## What you see

| Panel | What it shows | When it has nothing |
|---|---|---|
| Today's classes | Your classes today | Not built yet |
| Attendance to mark | The registers you still have to fill in | Not built yet |
| Assignments to grade | Submissions and quiz attempts waiting for your grade | "Nothing waiting to be graded" |
| Exam duties | Exams you invigilate or mark | Not built yet |
| Course roster | The courses you teach this term and who is in them | "No courses this term" |
| Squad overview | Your active athletes with their latest results, trend and fitness clearance | "No athletes yet" |
| Session plan | The plan for your training sessions | Not built yet |
| Videos to review | Recent training and match videos that nobody has annotated yet | "No videos waiting for review" |
| Upcoming bookings | Coming bookings of the facilities you approve | "No upcoming bookings" |
| Wearable alerts | Alerts from athletes' wearable devices | Not built yet |
| Tournament calendar | Published tournaments and their fixtures over the next four weeks | "Nothing on the calendar" |

## If a panel says its data is not available

Some panels say that their data is not available yet, or that you do not have access. Usually it means that part of
Education OS has not been switched on for your organisation yet. Tell your organisation admin. A panel for a part your
organisation has not switched on may also be missing altogether. Panels that say "Not built yet" are not finished by
the Education OS team, and nothing is wrong with your account.

## What you can do

### Know your squad

Squad overview lists each active athlete with the latest result, whether they are improving or declining and their
fitness clearance.

### Review videos

Videos to review lists recent training and match videos nobody has annotated yet.

### Approve bookings

Upcoming bookings lists coming bookings of the facilities you approve.

### Plan around tournaments

Tournament calendar lists published tournaments and their fixtures for the next four weeks.

### Grade student work

Assignments to grade lists submissions and quiz attempts waiting for you. When you have graded them all it says
Nothing waiting to be graded.

## Not sure?

Ask your head of department about your courses and exams, and your organisation admin if a panel is missing: your role
decides which panels you get.
