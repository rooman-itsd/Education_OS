# Organisation admin: getting started

You are the person who sets Education OS up for your institution. This guide is for you, not for developers. It follows
the order in which you will do things.

| Step | What you do | Read |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Register your organisation, confirm your email and wait for approval | [Registering and approval](registering-and-approval.md) |
| 2 | Add the people who will use Education OS and give each a role | [Users and roles](users-and-roles.md) |
| 3 | See which parts of Education OS you can use, check your plan and trial, and pay | [Modules, plan and billing](modules-plan-and-billing.md) |
| 4 | Understand your own dashboard | [Organisation Admin dashboard](../common-dashboards/org-admin.md) |

## Two places you work in

- **Your dashboard** at https://educationos.futureacad.ae. Sign in as the organisation admin. It shows your plan, your
  usage, the modules you can use, your users and recent changes.
- **The admin console** at https://educationos.futureacad.ae/admin.html. Sign in with the same email and password. It
  has the screens for users, roles, the module list and the audit log.

## What Education OS does not do for you yet

This guide only describes what you can do today. These are not available yet, so please do not look for them:

- Emailing an invitation to a new user. You set the user's first password and tell them yourself.
- Changing a person's role, switching a person off, or resetting a password, after the person has been added.
- Switching single modules on or off from a screen. Your academy type and your plan decide which modules you get.
- Changing your academy type from a screen.
- Changing the permissions of a role. The Roles and Permissions screen is for reading only.
- Help links inside the app. Use this guide, and the contacts below.

## Where to get help

- **For how to use a dashboard**: the [user guide index](../README.md) has a guide for each role. Share it with your staff
  and students.
- **For something wrong with your organisation's account, plan or approval**: write to the Education OS team that
  approved your registration. Organisations on the Premium plan get priority support.
- **For your own staff and students' questions**: if the helpdesk part of Education OS is switched on for you, your
  support staff run it. See [Support Staff](../common-dashboards/support-staff.md).
