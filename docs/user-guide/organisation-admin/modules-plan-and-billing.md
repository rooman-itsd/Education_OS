# Modules, plan and billing

## Which parts of Education OS you can use

Education OS is made of modules, such as admissions, examinations, the library or fee collection. Which of them your
organisation can use depends on two things:

- the **academy package** you chose when you registered, and
- your **plan**.

The Enabled modules panel on your dashboard shows the modules you can use now, grouped by suite. **Module Registry** in
the admin console lists every module and says what your plan includes.

A panel for a module that is not in your plan appears to you, and only to you, as a locked card that says
"Not in your plan. Upgrade to unlock". Your staff and students do not see it at all. This is why a person may tell you
that a panel is missing: the module behind it is not switched on for your organisation. To switch more modules on,
upgrade your plan. You cannot yet switch a single module on or off from a screen.

## The plans

| Plan | For | Price | Up to |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trial | Trying the common platform and one field-specific suite | Free for 30 days | 25 users, 500 students |
| Standard | Every common suite and your academy's field-specific suites | Rs 24,999 a month | 500 users, 10,000 students |
| Premium | Everything, every integration and priority support | Rs 59,999 a month | 5,000 users, 100,000 students |

These are the prices at the time of writing. The Plan and trial panel always shows the current ones.

## Check your plan and trial

The **Plan and trial** panel shows your plan, its price, when your trial ends or your plan renews, and a status:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Trial | You are in the free trial |
| Active | Your plan is paid and running |
| Payment overdue | A payment has not been received |
| Cancelled | The plan stops at the date shown ("Access until") |
| Read only | The trial has ended. You can look at your data but not change it |

When your trial ends and you have not moved to a paid plan, the organisation becomes read only. Upgrade before that day.

The **Usage against limits** panel shows how many users you have against the plan's limit, how many modules are on out of
those your academy has, and, once student records ship, how many students. Student counting is not working yet, so that
line shows no use.

## Upgrade or change plan

1. On your dashboard, find the Plan and trial panel. Under "Change plan" it lists the other plans with their price.
2. Choose **Switch** next to the plan you want. The top of your dashboard also has an **Unlock modules** button that
   starts an upgrade to the Standard plan.
3. You may be taken to a payment page. When the payment is confirmed, your new plan starts and more modules become
   available at once. Until then the panel says that payment for the plan is pending.

Only the organisation admin can change the plan.

## Invoices

The **Invoices** panel lists one invoice for each paid period, newest first. The trial is free, so it has none: the
first invoice is issued when a paid plan starts.

## Not available yet

- Cancelling a plan from a screen.
- Downloading an invoice as a PDF from your dashboard.
- Switching a single module on or off.
