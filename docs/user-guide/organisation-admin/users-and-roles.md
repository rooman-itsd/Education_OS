# Adding users and giving them roles

Every person who signs in to Education OS has a role. The role decides which dashboard they see. Each academy type has
its own roles, for example a designer, a coach or a research scholar, as well as the common ones such as student,
faculty and finance staff.

## Add a user

1. Open the admin console at https://educationos.futureacad.ae/admin.html and sign in as the organisation admin.
2. Choose **Users & Access**. It lists the people in your organisation. You can search the list and filter it by
   role, department and status.
3. Choose **Add User**.
4. Enter the person's full name and institutional email address.
5. Choose the **Assigned Role** from the list.
6. Enter an **Initial Password** of at least 8 characters.
7. Choose **Create User**. A message says the person can now sign in with that email.

Education OS does not email the password. Give it to the person yourself, and ask them to sign in at
https://educationos.futureacad.ae.

## Choose the right role

Pick the role that matches the person's work. Each role has its own guide:

- the academy's roles are in its guide in the [user guide index](../README.md);
- the common roles are in [Common dashboards](../common-dashboards/README.md).

A person gets one role when you add them, and the dashboard they see is the one for that role. People whose role is a
learner role see the student panels as well as their field's panels.

## Limits

Your plan limits how many users you can have. On the Trial plan it is 25. The Usage against limits panel on your
dashboard shows how many you use. See [Modules, plan and billing](modules-plan-and-billing.md).

## Not available yet

After a person has been added you cannot yet change their role, switch them off or reset their password from a screen.
Check the role and the email carefully before you create the user.

## Reading about roles

**Roles & Permissions** in the admin console lists the roles and what each can do. It is for reading. Changes you try
there are not saved.

## Keeping an eye on changes

The Recent activity panel on your dashboard, and **Audit Log** in the admin console, show who changed what in your
organisation, most recent first.
