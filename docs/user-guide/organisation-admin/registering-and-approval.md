# Registering your organisation

## Register

1. Open https://educationos.futureacad.ae and choose register. The page is called "Create your organisation".
2. **Academy package.** Choose the kind of institution you run. Your choice decides which roles and which parts of
   Education OS you get, and the words your institution uses. The [user guide index](../README.md) lists the academy
   types.
3. **Organisation name and short name.** The short name has 3 to 40 lowercase letters, numbers or hyphens. It is
   filled in for you from the name and you can change it. A few names, such as "admin" and "app", are reserved, and a
   name already taken is refused. Note it down: the sign-in page asks for it if your email address belongs to more
   than one organisation.
4. **Your details.** Your full name, your work email and a password of at least 8 characters. You become the
   organisation admin.
5. Tick the box to accept the Education OS terms of service on behalf of your organisation, then create the organisation.

You start on the free 30-day Trial plan.

## Confirm your email

Education OS sends a verification code, and a link, to your work email. Open the link, or type the code on the page.
The code works for 48 hours and once. If it has expired, register again.

## Wait for approval

After you confirm your email the page says "Waiting for approval". The Education OS team reviews your organisation and
the academy package you chose. When they approve it you get an email. Until then, signing in tells you that your
organisation's academic package is waiting for approval. Try again after the email arrives.

If your registration is not approved, the email to the address you registered says why.

## Sign in

Once approved, open https://educationos.futureacad.ae, choose sign in, and enter your email and password. You land on
your [Organisation Admin dashboard](../common-dashboards/org-admin.md).

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| The short name is already taken | Choose another one |
| The code is invalid, expired or used | Check the newest email, or register again if the first is over 48 hours old |
| The service could not send the email, or could not be reached | Try again a little later, and write to the Education OS team if it keeps happening |
| Sign-in says the package is waiting for approval | Wait for the approval email |
