# Farm Supervisor dashboard

You supervise farm and field work and teach. Your dashboard shows your classes, trainees to assess, bookings today,
low stock and active research trials.

## What you see

| Panel | What it shows | When it has nothing |
|---|---|---|
| Today's classes | Your classes today | Not built yet |
| Attendance to mark | The registers you still have to fill in | Not built yet |
| Assignments to grade | Submissions and quiz attempts waiting for your grade | "Nothing waiting to be graded" |
| Exam duties | Exams you invigilate or mark | Not built yet |
| Course roster | The courses you teach this term and who is in them | "No courses this term" |
| Trainees to assess | Students on a farm or practical rotation you supervise, so you can record how they are doing | Not built yet |
| Bookings today | Today's bookings of the farm plots and labs you look after | "No bookings today" |
| Low stock | Items that have fallen to their reorder level or run out | "Stock is healthy" |
| Active projects | Approved research trials that teams are working on, ending soonest first | "No active projects" |

## If a panel says its data is not available

Some panels say that their data is not available yet, or that you do not have access. Usually it means that part of
Education OS has not been switched on for your organisation yet. Tell your organisation admin. A panel for a part your
organisation has not switched on may also be missing altogether. Panels that say "Not built yet" are not finished by
the Education OS team, and nothing is wrong with your account.

## What you can do

### Assess trainees

Trainees to assess lists students on a farm or practical rotation you supervise, so you can record how they are doing.

### See today's bookings

Bookings today lists the farm plots and labs booked today, with counts, so you can have them ready.

### Reorder in time

Low stock lists items at or below their reorder level. Tell the person who raises purchase orders, or raise one
yourself if you can.

### Watch active research trials

Active projects lists approved research trials, ending soonest first, with milestones accepted so far.

### Grade student work

Assignments to grade lists submissions and quiz attempts waiting for you. When you have graded them all it says
Nothing waiting to be graded.

## Not sure?

Ask your head of department about your courses and exams, and your organisation admin if a panel is missing: your role
decides which panels you get.
