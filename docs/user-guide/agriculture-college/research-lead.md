# Research Lead dashboard

You lead research trials. Your dashboard shows active trials, trials waiting for review, extension activities coming
up and open rotation places.

## What you see

| Panel | What it shows | When it has nothing |
|---|---|---|
| Active projects | Approved research trials that teams are working on, ending soonest first | "No active projects" |
| To review | Work submitted by the teams you guide: milestones and final submissions waiting for your review | "Nothing waiting for your review" |
| Upcoming productions | The next extension activities | "Nothing coming up" |
| Open farm or practical rotations | Farm or practical rotations that still have places | Not built yet |

## If a panel says its data is not available

Some panels say that their data is not available yet, or that you do not have access. Usually it means that part of
Education OS has not been switched on for your organisation yet. Tell your organisation admin. A panel for a part your
organisation has not switched on may also be missing altogether. Panels that say "Not built yet" are not finished by
the Education OS team, and nothing is wrong with your account.

## What you can do

### Watch active research trials

Active projects lists approved research trials, ending soonest first, with milestones accepted so far.

### Review research trials

To review lists milestones and final submissions from the teams you guide, so you can accept them or return them with
comments.

### Know what is coming

Upcoming productions lists the next ones with their dates.

## Not sure?

Ask your head or manager about the work itself, and your organisation admin if a panel is missing: your role decides
which panels you get.
