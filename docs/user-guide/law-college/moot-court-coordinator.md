# Moot Court Coordinator dashboard

You run moot court and competitions. Your dashboard shows open selection calls, the competition calendar and the next
competitions, and open internship places.

## What you see

| Panel | What it shows | When it has nothing |
|---|---|---|
| Open Calls | Moot court selections that are open for applications | "No open calls right now" |
| Production calendar | Competitions and debates scheduled over the coming weeks | "Nothing scheduled" |
| Upcoming productions | The next competitions and debates | "Nothing coming up" |
| Open legal internships and clinics | Legal internships and clinics that still have places | Not built yet |

## If a panel says its data is not available

Some panels say that their data is not available yet, or that you do not have access. Usually it means that part of
Education OS has not been switched on for your organisation yet. Tell your organisation admin. A panel for a part your
organisation has not switched on may also be missing altogether. Panels that say "Not built yet" are not finished by
the Education OS team, and nothing is wrong with your account.

## What you can do

### Keep calls open and clear

Open Calls lists the moot court selections that are open for applications.

### Plan around the calendar

Production calendar lists competitions and debates with their dates over the coming weeks.

### Know what is coming

Upcoming productions lists the next ones with their dates.

## Not sure?

Ask your head or manager about the work itself, and your organisation admin if a panel is missing: your role decides
which panels you get.
