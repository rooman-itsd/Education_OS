# Equipment Manager dashboard

You look after cameras, lights and other equipment. Your dashboard shows low stock, overdue loans, today's bookings
and maintenance tickets.

## What you see

| Panel | What it shows | When it has nothing |
|---|---|---|
| Low stock | Equipment that have fallen to their reorder level or run out | "Stock is healthy" |
| Overdue loans | Loans that were not returned by their due date | "No overdue loans" |
| Bookings today | Today's bookings of the edit suites and studios you look after | "No bookings today" |
| Maintenance tickets | Open maintenance tickets, those past their service target and urgent ones first | "No open tickets" |

## If a panel says its data is not available

Some panels say that their data is not available yet, or that you do not have access. Usually it means that part of
Education OS has not been switched on for your organisation yet. Tell your organisation admin. A panel for a part your
organisation has not switched on may also be missing altogether. Panels that say "Not built yet" are not finished by
the Education OS team, and nothing is wrong with your account.

## What you can do

### Reorder in time

Low stock lists equipment at or below their reorder level. Tell the person who raises purchase orders, or raise one
yourself if you can.

### Chase overdue loans

Overdue loans lists what was not returned by its due date, so you can ask for it back.

### See today's bookings

Bookings today lists the edit suites and studios booked today, with counts, so you can have them ready.

### Work through tickets

Maintenance tickets lists open tickets, with those past their service target and urgent ones first.

## Not sure?

Ask your head or manager about the work itself, and your organisation admin if a panel is missing: your role decides
which panels you get.
