# Filmmaker dashboard

You are a film or media student. Your dashboard shows your classes and fees, your film projects and their milestones,
your equipment loans, your showreel and the next screenings.

## What you see

| Panel | What it shows | When it has nothing |
|---|---|---|
| Today | Your classes today | Not built yet |
| Attendance summary | How much of your attendance is recorded | Not built yet |
| Upcoming assessments | Your next assessments and exams | Not built yet |
| Course materials | Notes, links and documents your teachers recently published in your courses | "No new course materials" |
| Fee dues | Fees you still owe | "No fees due" |
| Hostel notices | Notices for your hostel and for all hostels | "No hostel notices right now" |
| Transport notices | Delays, cancellations and route changes for your bus and all routes | "No transport notices right now" |
| Library loans | Books you have borrowed, with due dates and overdue ones marked | "No books on loan" |
| My projects | Your film projects, with progress and the next milestone | A prompt that you have no film projects yet |
| Milestones due | Milestones of your active film projects that are due in the next two weeks or overdue | "Nothing due in the next two weeks" |
| My bookings | The edit suites and studios you have booked, coming up, with day, time and status | "No upcoming bookings" |
| My loans | Equipment you have borrowed, from awaiting approval to overdue | "Nothing on loan" |
| My Portfolio | The showreels you have added and submitted | "No portfolio items yet" |
| Upcoming productions | The next screenings and festivals | "Nothing coming up" |

## If a panel says its data is not available

Some panels say that their data is not available yet, or that you do not have access. Usually it means that part of
Education OS has not been switched on for your organisation yet. Tell your organisation admin. A panel for a part your
organisation has not switched on may also be missing altogether. Panels that say "Not built yet" are not finished by
the Education OS team, and nothing is wrong with your account.

## What you can do

### Follow your film projects

My projects shows each film project, how far it has come and the next milestone. Open it to see what has been accepted
or returned.

### Meet your milestones

Milestones due lists what falls in the next two weeks and anything overdue or returned. Check it at the start of every
week.

### Book an edit suite or studio

An edit suite or studio you book shows in My bookings with its day, time and status in words. Only edit suites and
studios that your facility staff have set up can be booked. If the one you need is missing, ask the facility staff to
add it.

### Track what you have borrowed

My loans shows each loan from awaiting approval to ready to collect, on loan, part returned or overdue. Return
equipment before the due date.

### Keep your showreels up to date

Add each showreel to your portfolio. Your teacher reviews what you submit, and My Portfolio lists what you have added
so far.

## Not sure?

Ask your teacher or supervisor about your work, the facility staff about bookings and loans, the accounts office about
fees, and your organisation admin about anything on your dashboard that looks wrong or missing.
