# Management dashboard

You are the vice chancellor, principal or registrar. Your dashboard gives a summary of admissions, fee collection,
attendance and results, grievances, compliance and events.

## What you see

| Panel | What it shows | When it has nothing |
|---|---|---|
| Admissions funnel | How many applied, are eligible, were offered a seat and accepted, stage by stage | "No applications yet" |
| Collection vs target | Fees collected each month against the target | "No collection targets set" |
| Attendance & Results by Department | Average attendance and pass rate for each department | "No departmental metrics" |
| Open grievances | Grievances still open | Not built yet |
| Compliance calendar | Compliance dates coming up | Not built yet |
| Upcoming productions | The next productions | "Nothing coming up" |

## If a panel says its data is not available

Some panels say that their data is not available yet, or that you do not have access. Usually it means that part of
Education OS has not been switched on for your organisation yet. Tell your organisation admin. A panel for a part your
organisation has not switched on may also be missing altogether. Panels that say "Not built yet" are not finished by
the Education OS team, and nothing is wrong with your account.

## What you can do

### Start each day with your panels

Open your dashboard each morning and read each panel from the top. They show the few things you most need today.

### Know what is coming

Upcoming productions lists the next ones with their dates.

## Not sure?

Ask your head or manager about the work itself, and your organisation admin if a panel is missing: your role decides
which panels you get.
