# Organisation Admin dashboard

You are the organisation admin. Your dashboard shows your plan and trial, how much of your plan you use, the modules
you can use, your users and their roles, invoices and recent changes.

## What you see

| Panel | What it shows | When it has nothing |
|---|---|---|
| Plan and trial | Your plan, its price, when the trial ends or the plan renews, and the other plans you can switch to | Always shows your plan |
| Usage against limits | How many users, modules and students you use against what your plan allows | Always shows your usage |
| Enabled modules | The modules your organisation can use now, grouped by suite | "No modules are on" |
| Users and roles | The people in your organisation and how many hold each role | "No users yet" |
| Invoices | Your invoices, one for each paid period | No invoices yet (the trial is free; the first invoice comes when a paid plan starts) |
| Recent activity | Who changed what in your organisation, most recent first | "Nothing recorded yet" |

## What you can do

### Get your organisation set up

The [organisation admin getting-started guide](../organisation-admin/README.md) takes you through registering, adding
users and giving them roles, and choosing and paying for a plan.

### Add a person

Users and roles shows who you have added. Add people, and choose each person's role, in the admin console. See [Users
and roles](../organisation-admin/users-and-roles.md).

### Watch your trial and your limits

Plan and trial and Usage against limits tell you when your trial ends and how close you are to your plan's limits. See
[Modules, plan and billing](../organisation-admin/modules-plan-and-billing.md).

### Check what changed

Recent activity lists who changed what, most recent first.

## Not sure?

If a panel says that its data is not available, or you think something is wrong with your account or plan, write to
the Education OS team that approved your registration.
