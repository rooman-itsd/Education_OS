# Super Admin dashboard

You are the platform operator. Your dashboard shows organisations waiting for approval, organisations by status, new
registrations, trials ending, revenue, subscriptions and platform actions.

## What you see

| Panel | What it shows | When it has nothing |
|---|---|---|
| Approval queue | New registrations and package changes waiting for your approval, oldest first | "Nothing waiting for approval" |
| Organisations by status | How many organisations are in each status | "No organisations yet" |
| Recent registrations | The five newest organisations, with package, plan and status | "No registrations yet" |
| Trials ending soon | Trials that have ended or end within 14 days, soonest first | A list of the organisations concerned |
| Revenue by plan | Monthly recurring revenue and money received in the last 30 days, for each plan | "No paid subscriptions yet" |
| Subscriptions by plan | How many organisations are on each plan, by status | "No subscriptions yet" |
| Super Admin actions | Platform-level administrative actions, across organisations | "No platform actions yet" |

## What you can do

### Approve a registration

Approval queue and Recent registrations only show what is waiting. Approve or reject a registration in the admin
console at https://educationos.futureacad.ae/admin.html: on its Dashboard, the Organisations awaiting approval panel
lets you review an organisation and then approve it, or reject it with a reason. The organisation can sign in once it
is approved.

### Follow trials and revenue

Trials ending soon lists trials that have ended or end within 14 days. Revenue by plan and Subscriptions by plan show
the money and the count for each plan.

### Check platform actions

Super Admin actions lists platform-level changes, across organisations.
