# Student dashboard

You are a student. Your dashboard shows your timetable, attendance, exams, course material, fees, hostel and transport
notices and library books.

## What you see

| Panel | What it shows | When it has nothing |
|---|---|---|
| Today | Your classes today | Not built yet |
| Attendance summary | How much of your attendance is recorded | Not built yet |
| Upcoming assessments | Your next assessments and exams | Not built yet |
| Course materials | Notes, links and documents your teachers recently published in your courses | "No new course materials" |
| Fee dues | Fees you still owe | "No fees due" |
| Hostel notices | Notices for your hostel and for all hostels | "No hostel notices right now" |
| Transport notices | Delays, cancellations and route changes for your bus and all routes | "No transport notices right now" |
| Library loans | Books you have borrowed, with due dates and overdue ones marked | "No books on loan" |

In an academy such as a design institute or a sports college, a student or teacher may have more panels than these.
Your academy's guide lists them.

## If a panel says its data is not available

Some panels say that their data is not available yet, or that you do not have access. Usually it means that part of
Education OS has not been switched on for your organisation yet. Tell your organisation admin. A panel for a part your
organisation has not switched on may also be missing altogether. Panels that say "Not built yet" are not finished by
the Education OS team, and nothing is wrong with your account.

## What you can do

### Find new material

Course materials lists what your teachers published recently. Open the course for older material.

### Check what you owe

Look at Fee dues first. If it lists a fee, pay it at the accounts office or online if your institution has switched on
online payment; if you think an amount is wrong, ask the accounts office.

### Return books on time

Library loans lists your books with due dates. Overdue books are marked, so return or renew them at the library.

## Not sure?

Ask your teacher or supervisor about your work, the facility staff about bookings and loans, the accounts office about
fees, and your organisation admin about anything on your dashboard that looks wrong or missing.
