# Common dashboards: user guide

These dashboards are the same at every kind of institution. This guide is for the people who use them, not for the
people who build them. There is one page for each role:

| You are | Read |
|---|---|
| Any student | [Student](student.md) |
| Any teacher or instructor | [Faculty / Instructor](faculty.md) |
| A head of department or department administrator | [Department Admin / HoD](department-admin.md) |
| Staff in the examination office | [Examination Staff](examination-staff.md) |
| Staff who look after rooms, stores and assets | [Facility / Inventory Staff](facility-staff.md) |
| Staff in the accounts office | [Finance Staff](finance-staff.md) |
| Staff in human resources | [HR Staff](hr-staff.md) |

| The platform operator | [Super Admin](super-admin.md) |

Students and teachers in an academy such as a design institute or a sports college have extra panels for their field.
Their academy's guide, listed in the [user guide index](../README.md), covers them.

## Signing in

1. Open https://educationos.futureacad.ae and choose sign in.
2. Enter the email address and password your organisation admin set up for you.
3. You land on your own dashboard. Its heading shows your organisation's name and your role, for example
   "Student dashboard".

If you cannot sign in, ask your organisation admin to check that your account exists and that the organisation has
been approved. If you see that your organisation's academic package is waiting for approval, try again once the
approval email has come.

## How your dashboard works

- Your dashboard is a set of panels. Each panel has a title and shows the few things you most need today.
- A panel that has nothing to show says so in plain words, for example "No upcoming bookings". That is normal for a
  new organisation. It does not mean something is broken.
- Panels appear as your organisation switches on each part of Education OS. If a panel you expect is missing, it has
  not been switched on for your organisation yet; ask your organisation admin.
- If a panel says its data is not available, or that you do not have access, tell your organisation admin. They can
  check your role.
- A panel that shows a plain grey card with "Not built yet" is a part the Education OS team has not finished. It is
  not a fault with your account.
