# Applicant dashboard

You have applied, or are applying, to the institution. Your dashboard shows your application, any documents waiting to
be checked, your application fee and announcements.

## What you see

| Panel | What it shows | When it has nothing |
|---|---|---|
| Application status | Your application and offer status. If it is empty, you can link your submitted application with its reference number and email | A prompt to link your submitted application |
| Pending documents | Documents you have uploaded that are waiting for someone to verify them | "Nothing waiting for verification" |
| Fee due | The application fee you still have to pay | "No fees due" |
| Announcements | Current notices published by your organisation | "No announcements" |

## If a panel says its data is not available

Some panels say that their data is not available yet, or that you do not have access. Usually it means that part of
Education OS has not been switched on for your organisation yet. Tell your organisation admin. A panel for a part your
organisation has not switched on may also be missing altogether. Panels that say "Not built yet" are not finished by
the Education OS team, and nothing is wrong with your account.

## What you can do

### Link your application

If Application status is empty, enter the reference number and email of your submitted application to link it to your account. Then Pending documents shows what still has to be verified.

## Not sure?

Ask the admissions office about your application and documents, the accounts office about the application fee, and your
organisation admin if a panel looks wrong or is missing.
