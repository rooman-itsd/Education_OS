# Exhibition & Gallery Manager dashboard

You manage exhibitions and the gallery. Your dashboard shows the exhibition calendar, the next exhibitions, today's
bookings and low stock of materials.

## What you see

| Panel | What it shows | When it has nothing |
|---|---|---|
| Production calendar | Exhibitions scheduled over the coming weeks | "Nothing scheduled" |
| Upcoming productions | The next exhibitions | "Nothing coming up" |
| Bookings today | Today's bookings of the studios you look after | "No bookings today" |
| Low stock | Materials that have fallen to their reorder level or run out | "Stock is healthy" |

## If a panel says its data is not available

Some panels say that their data is not available yet, or that you do not have access. Usually it means that part of
Education OS has not been switched on for your organisation yet. Tell your organisation admin. A panel for a part your
organisation has not switched on may also be missing altogether. Panels that say "Not built yet" are not finished by
the Education OS team, and nothing is wrong with your account.

## What you can do

### Plan around the calendar

Production calendar lists exhibitions with their dates over the coming weeks.

### Know what is coming

Upcoming productions lists the next ones with their dates.

### See today's bookings

Bookings today lists the studios booked today, with counts, so you can have them ready.

### Reorder in time

Low stock lists materials at or below their reorder level. Tell the person who raises purchase orders, or raise one
yourself if you can.

## Not sure?

Ask your head or manager about the work itself, and your organisation admin if a panel is missing: your role decides
which panels you get.
