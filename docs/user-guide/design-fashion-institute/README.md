# Education OS for a design or fashion institute: user guide

This guide is for the people who use the dashboards, not for the people who build them. There is one page for each
role:

| You are | Read |
|---|---|
| A student designing collections, projects and shows | [Designer](designer.md) |
| A teacher who reviews collections and judges critiques | [Design Mentor](design-mentor.md) |
| The person who builds links with fashion houses, studios and employers | [Industry Liaison](industry-liaison.md) |

## Signing in

1. Open https://educationos.futureacad.ae and choose sign in.
2. Enter the email address and password your organisation admin set up for you.
3. You land on your own dashboard. Its heading shows your institute's name and your role, for example
   "Designer dashboard".

If you cannot sign in, ask your organisation admin to check that your account exists and that the institute has been
approved.

## How your dashboard works

- Your dashboard is a set of panels. Each panel has a title and shows the few things you most need today.
- A panel that has nothing to show says so in plain words, for example "No upcoming bookings". That is normal for a
  new institute. It does not mean something is broken.
- Panels appear as your institute switches on each part of Education OS. If a panel you expect is missing, it has not
  been switched on for your institute yet; ask your organisation admin.
- If a panel says its data is not available, or that you do not have access, tell your organisation admin. They can
  check your role.

## Words your institute uses

Your institute uses its own words in place of the general ones:

| In Education OS | At a design institute |
|---|---|
| Portfolio item | Collection |
| Project | Collection or industry project |
| Selection round | Critique |
| Production | Show |
| Bookable resource | Studio |
| Placement | Internship |
