# Industry Liaison dashboard

You connect the institute with fashion houses, studios and employers. Your dashboard shows internships still looking
for students, the projects running with industry, the calendar of shows and the job openings.

## What you see

| Panel | What it shows | When it is empty |
|---|---|---|
| Placements open | Internships that still need a student | Appears once internships are switched on |
| Active projects | Collection and industry projects now running | Appears once projects are switched on |
| Production calendar | Shows scheduled over the coming weeks | Appears once shows are switched on |
| Placement openings | Job openings, applications received and students placed, with the openings that close soonest | "No openings yet" |

## What you can do

### Find internships that need students

Placements open is your list of internships with no student yet. Use it to decide which company to call first.

### Watch the openings

Placement openings counts the open openings, the applications and the students placed. Under "Closing soonest" it
lists the openings with the nearest closing date and how many applications each has, so you can chase the ones that
need more candidates.

### Plan around shows

The Production calendar shows when shows fall, so you can invite industry guests and avoid clashes with deadlines.

## Not sure?

Ask your organisation admin if a panel is missing: your role decides which panels you get, and a panel appears only
once its part of Education OS is switched on for your institute.
