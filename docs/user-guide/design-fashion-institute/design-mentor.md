# Design Mentor dashboard

You teach, guide and assess at a design or fashion institute. Your dashboard shows your classes, the collections
waiting for your review and the critiques you judge.

## What you see

| Panel | What it shows | When it is empty |
|---|---|---|
| Today's classes, Attendance to mark | Your classes today and the registers you still have to fill in | Appears once timetables are set up |
| Assignments to grade | Student work waiting for a grade | Appears once your courses start |
| Exam duties | Exams you invigilate or mark | Appears once exams are scheduled |
| Course roster | The courses you teach this term and who is in them | "No courses this term" |
| Reviews Pending | Collections students have submitted for your review | "No reviews pending" |
| Projects to review | Collection and industry projects waiting for you | Appears once projects are switched on |
| To Judge | Students waiting to be judged in a critique | "No candidates awaiting review" |
| Trainees to assess | Interns you supervise and assess | Appears once internships are switched on |

## What you can do

### Review collections

Reviews Pending lists what students have submitted. An empty panel means nothing is waiting for you.

### Judge a critique

When you are on a critique panel, To Judge lists the students whose work you have to score. Once you have judged
them all, it says "No candidates awaiting review".

### Assess interns

Trainees to assess lists the students on an internship you supervise, so you can record how they are doing.

## Not sure?

Ask your organisation admin if a panel is missing: your role decides which panels you get.
