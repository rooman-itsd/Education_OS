# Designer dashboard

You are a student at a design or fashion institute. Your dashboard shows your timetable, your fees and your
studio bookings, and where your collections and internship stand.

## What you see

| Panel | What it shows | When it is empty |
|---|---|---|
| Today, Attendance summary | Your classes today and how much of your attendance is recorded | Appears once timetables are set up |
| Upcoming assessments | Your next assessments and exams | Appears once exams are scheduled |
| Course materials | Material your teachers share | Appears once your courses start |
| Fee dues | Fees you still owe | "No fees due" |
| Hostel notices, Transport notices | Notices for the hostel or the bus you use | "No hostel notices right now", "No transport notices right now" |
| Library loans | Books you have borrowed | "No books on loan" |
| My Portfolio | The collections you have added | "No portfolio items yet" |
| My projects | Your collections and industry projects in progress | Appears once projects are switched on |
| Upcoming productions | The next shows | "Nothing coming up" |
| My bookings | Studios you have booked, coming up | "No upcoming bookings" |
| My placement | Your internship | Appears once internships are switched on |
| Upcoming Selection Events | Critiques you are due to attend | "No upcoming selection rounds" |

## What you can do

### Check what is due

Open your dashboard each morning. Look at Fee dues first, then Upcoming Selection Events and My bookings, so you
know what is coming this week.

### Book a studio

A studio you book shows in My bookings with its day and time, and its status in words. Only studios your facility
team has set up can be booked. If the studio you need is not offered, ask your facility staff to add it.

### Keep your portfolio up to date

Add each collection to your portfolio. Your design mentor reviews what you submit. My Portfolio lists what you have added so far.

## Not sure?

Ask your design mentor about collections and critiques, your facility staff about studios, and your organisation admin
about anything on your dashboard that looks wrong or missing.
