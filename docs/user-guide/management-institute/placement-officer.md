# Placement Officer dashboard

You run placements. Your dashboard shows open internship places, placement openings and applications, upcoming campus
drives and the events calendar.

## What you see

| Panel | What it shows | When it has nothing |
|---|---|---|
| Open internships | Internships that still have places | Not built yet |
| Placement openings | Open job openings, applications received and students placed | "No openings yet" |
| Upcoming drives | Campus recruitment drives in the next 30 days | "No drives scheduled" |
| Production calendar | Case competitions and industry events scheduled over the coming weeks | "Nothing scheduled" |

## If a panel says its data is not available

Some panels say that their data is not available yet, or that you do not have access. Usually it means that part of
Education OS has not been switched on for your organisation yet. Tell your organisation admin. A panel for a part your
organisation has not switched on may also be missing altogether. Panels that say "Not built yet" are not finished by
the Education OS team, and nothing is wrong with your account.

## What you can do

### Follow openings

Placement openings shows open openings, applications received, students placed and the openings closing soonest.

### Prepare for drives

Upcoming drives lists campus drives in the next 30 days.

### Plan around the calendar

Production calendar lists case competitions and industry events with their dates over the coming weeks.

## Not sure?

Ask your head or manager about the work itself, and your organisation admin if a panel is missing: your role decides
which panels you get.
