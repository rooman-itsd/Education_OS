# Corporate Mentor dashboard

You mentor students from industry. Your dashboard shows your classes, live projects to review, interns to assess,
competition selections to judge and events in preparation.

## What you see

| Panel | What it shows | When it has nothing |
|---|---|---|
| Today's classes | Your classes today | Not built yet |
| Attendance to mark | The registers you still have to fill in | Not built yet |
| Assignments to grade | Submissions and quiz attempts waiting for your grade | "Nothing waiting to be graded" |
| Exam duties | Exams you invigilate or mark | Not built yet |
| Course roster | The courses you teach this term and who is in them | "No courses this term" |
| To review | Work submitted by the teams you guide: milestones and final submissions waiting for your review | "Nothing waiting for your review" |
| Trainees to assess | Students on an internship you supervise, so you can record how they are doing | Not built yet |
| To Judge | Candidates waiting for your score or decision in a competition selection | "No candidates awaiting review" |
| In preparation | Case competitions and industry events being got ready, with what is still outstanding | "Nothing in preparation" |

## If a panel says its data is not available

Some panels say that their data is not available yet, or that you do not have access. Usually it means that part of
Education OS has not been switched on for your organisation yet. Tell your organisation admin. A panel for a part your
organisation has not switched on may also be missing altogether. Panels that say "Not built yet" are not finished by
the Education OS team, and nothing is wrong with your account.

## What you can do

### Review live projects

To review lists milestones and final submissions from the teams you guide, so you can accept them or return them with
comments.

### Assess trainees

Trainees to assess lists students on an internship you supervise, so you can record how they are doing.

### Judge a competition selection

When you are on a panel, To Judge lists the candidates whose work you have to score. When you have judged them all, it
says No candidates awaiting review.

### Get case competitions and industry events ready

In preparation lists each case competition or industry event being got ready with what is still outstanding.

### Grade student work

Assignments to grade lists submissions and quiz attempts waiting for you. When you have graded them all it says
Nothing waiting to be graded.

## Not sure?

Ask your head of department about your courses and exams, and your organisation admin if a panel is missing: your role
decides which panels you get.
