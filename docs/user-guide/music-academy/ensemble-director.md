# Ensemble / Orchestra Director dashboard

You direct an ensemble or orchestra. Your dashboard shows what is in preparation, the concert calendar, open audition
calls and low stock of instruments.

## What you see

| Panel | What it shows | When it has nothing |
|---|---|---|
| In preparation | Recitals and concerts being got ready, with what is still outstanding | "Nothing in preparation" |
| Production calendar | Recitals and concerts scheduled over the coming weeks | "Nothing scheduled" |
| Open Calls | Auditions that are open for applications | "No open calls right now" |
| Low stock | Instruments that have fallen to their reorder level or run out | "Stock is healthy" |

## If a panel says its data is not available

Some panels say that their data is not available yet, or that you do not have access. Usually it means that part of
Education OS has not been switched on for your organisation yet. Tell your organisation admin. A panel for a part your
organisation has not switched on may also be missing altogether. Panels that say "Not built yet" are not finished by
the Education OS team, and nothing is wrong with your account.

## What you can do

### Get recitals and concerts ready

In preparation lists each recital or concert being got ready with what is still outstanding.

### Plan around the calendar

Production calendar lists recitals and concerts with their dates over the coming weeks.

### Keep calls open and clear

Open Calls lists the auditions that are open for applications.

### Reorder in time

Low stock lists instruments at or below their reorder level. Tell the person who raises purchase orders, or raise one
yourself if you can.

## Not sure?

Ask your head or manager about the work itself, and your organisation admin if a panel is missing: your role decides
which panels you get.
