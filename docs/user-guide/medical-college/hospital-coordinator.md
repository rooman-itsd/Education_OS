# Hospital Coordinator dashboard

You coordinate with partner hospitals. Your dashboard shows rotations under way, open rotation places, today's
bookings and reports that are due.

## What you see

| Panel | What it shows | When it has nothing |
|---|---|---|
| Active clinical rotations | Clinical rotations that are under way now | Not built yet |
| Open clinical rotations | Clinical rotations that still have places | Not built yet |
| Bookings today | Today's bookings of the labs and skills rooms you look after | "No bookings today" |
| Reports due | Statutory reports that are due | Not built yet |

## If a panel says its data is not available

Some panels say that their data is not available yet, or that you do not have access. Usually it means that part of
Education OS has not been switched on for your organisation yet. Tell your organisation admin. A panel for a part your
organisation has not switched on may also be missing altogether. Panels that say "Not built yet" are not finished by
the Education OS team, and nothing is wrong with your account.

## What you can do

### Start each day with your panels

Open your dashboard each morning and read each panel from the top. They show the few things you most need today.

### See today's bookings

Bookings today lists the labs and skills rooms booked today, with counts, so you can have them ready.

## Not sure?

Ask your head or manager about the work itself, and your organisation admin if a panel is missing: your role decides
which panels you get.
