# Research Scholar dashboard

You are a PhD or research student. Your dashboard shows your timetable and fees like any student, and where your
research projects, thesis milestones, publications, applications and grants stand.

## What you see

| Panel | What it shows | When it has nothing |
|---|---|---|
| Today | Your classes today | Not built yet |
| Attendance summary | How much of your attendance is recorded | Not built yet |
| Upcoming assessments | Your next assessments and exams | Not built yet |
| Course materials | Notes, links and documents your teachers recently published in your courses | "No new course materials" |
| Fee dues | Fees you still owe | "No fees due" |
| Hostel notices | Notices for your hostel and for all hostels | "No hostel notices right now" |
| Transport notices | Delays, cancellations and route changes for your bus and all routes | "No transport notices right now" |
| Library loans | Books you have borrowed, with due dates and overdue ones marked | "No books on loan" |
| My projects | Your research projects, with progress and the next milestone | A prompt that you have no research projects yet |
| Milestones due | Milestones of your active research projects that are due in the next two weeks or overdue | "Nothing due in the next two weeks" |
| My Portfolio | The publications you have added and submitted | "No portfolio items yet" |
| Upcoming productions | The next conferences | "Nothing coming up" |
| My Applications | Your applications and the stage each has reached | "No active applications" |
| My grants | Grants where you are an investigator, and whether a utilisation certificate is due | "No grants yet" |

## If a panel says its data is not available

Some panels say that their data is not available yet, or that you do not have access. Usually it means that part of
Education OS has not been switched on for your organisation yet. Tell your organisation admin. A panel for a part your
organisation has not switched on may also be missing altogether. Panels that say "Not built yet" are not finished by
the Education OS team, and nothing is wrong with your account.

## What you can do

### Follow your research projects

My projects shows each research project, how far it has come and the next milestone. Open it to see what has been
accepted or returned.

### Meet your milestones

Milestones due lists what falls in the next two weeks and anything overdue or returned. Check it at the start of every
week.

### Keep your publications up to date

Add each publication to your portfolio. Your teacher reviews what you submit, and My Portfolio lists what you have
added so far.

### Know what is coming

Upcoming productions lists the next ones with their dates.

### Follow your applications

My Applications shows each application and the stage it has reached.

## Not sure?

Ask your teacher or supervisor about your work, the facility staff about bookings and loans, the accounts office about
fees, and your organisation admin about anything on your dashboard that looks wrong or missing.
