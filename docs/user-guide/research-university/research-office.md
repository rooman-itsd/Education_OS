# Research Office dashboard

You run the university's research office. Your dashboard shows open calls, active research projects, grant budgets,
the conference calendar and reports that are due.

## What you see

| Panel | What it shows | When it has nothing |
|---|---|---|
| Open Calls | Supervisor allocations and vivas that are open for applications | "No open calls right now" |
| Active projects | Approved research projects that teams are working on, ending soonest first | "No active projects" |
| Budget vs spend | For each grant or budget head: what was allocated, committed and is still available | "No grants to show" |
| Production calendar | Conferences scheduled over the coming weeks | "Nothing scheduled" |
| Reports due | Statutory reports that are due | Not built yet |

## If a panel says its data is not available

Some panels say that their data is not available yet, or that you do not have access. Usually it means that part of
Education OS has not been switched on for your organisation yet. Tell your organisation admin. A panel for a part your
organisation has not switched on may also be missing altogether. Panels that say "Not built yet" are not finished by
the Education OS team, and nothing is wrong with your account.

## What you can do

### Keep calls open and clear

Open Calls lists the supervisor allocations and vivas that are open for applications.

### Watch active research projects

Active projects lists approved research projects, ending soonest first, with milestones accepted so far.

### Check grants against spending

Budget vs spend shows, for each grant or head, what is allocated, committed and still available.

### Plan around the calendar

Production calendar lists conferences with their dates over the coming weeks.

## Not sure?

Ask your head or manager about the work itself, and your organisation admin if a panel is missing: your role decides
which panels you get.
